How to Get Started with Breathwork

A practical guide to building a breathwork practice. Recorded live with Nick Heath, Ph.D.

Part 1Part 2Part 3

The workshop is split into three parts, about 90 minutes in total. Watch them in order. Part 3 includes a guided breathing practice, so find a comfortable seat before you start it.

Part 1 • 30 min

Why breathwork, its benefits, the three main types of breathing, and nasal breathing.

Part 2 • 30 min

Diaphragmatic breathing, safety, a 2-minute slow breathing reset, and choosing classes and apps.

Part 3 • 28 min

How long and when to practice, building a daily habit, and a guided 12-minute breath scan.
