The Science

Why Breathing Works

Breathing isn't a cure-all. But it is one of the most direct levers we have for influencing the nervous system.

Anxiety is not just a mindset issue. It's a nervous system pattern. When the body stays in sympathetic overdrive, heart rate variability drops, brainwave patterns shift, and the system remains on alert.

Slow, structured breathing increases parasympathetic activity, alters communication across brain regions, and restores the body's capacity to settle.
