7-Day Calm Reset

Day 1: Your First Minute

One minute of slow breathing. That's all it takes to start shifting your nervous system.

Day 1 of 7

Welcome to the 7-Day Calm Reset. Today's practice is simple: one minute of slow breathing. That's it.

Research shows even a single session of slow breathing activates your parasympathetic nervous system — the branch responsible for calm and recovery. You don't need to believe it yet. Just press play.

The audio below will guide you through one minute of 4-6 breathing: four counts in, six counts out. Breathe through your nose if you can. Let your belly rise before your chest.
