Slow Breathing & Anxiety

The research on slow, controlled breathing for stress, anxiety, and nervous system regulation — explained in plain English.

Slowing the breath to around 6 breaths per minute activates the parasympathetic nervous system, improves heart rate variability, and reduces anxiety across dozens of peer-reviewed studies. This collection covers the science of resonance breathing, practical protocols, and the physiology behind why a slow exhale really does calm you down.
