Breathing & the Brain

How breathing patterns shape cognition, creativity, memory, focus, and the brain's electrical rhythms.

Breathing and brain function are more tightly coupled than most people realize. Respiratory rhythm entrains neural oscillations, modulates the amygdala, and drives cerebrospinal fluid flow. Practical upshots: nasal breathing sharpens memory, slow breathing reduces fear responses, and even a few deep breaths can shift you into a more focused state. These articles trace the research.
